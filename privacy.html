ShortsPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ShortsPilot ("the app") is operated by Riyaz Ahmed Anesur, India. Contact: riyazanesur.mca@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Text prompts you type. When you press Generate, your prompt, chosen duration and aspect ratio are sent to our server to create a scene.
  • Anonymous app user ID. Our subscription provider (RevenueCat) assigns a random ID to your install so we can recognise your subscription. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Subscription and purchase status. Handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Technical logs. Our server records IP address, request time and errors for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data. The app has no accounts and shows no ads.

How we use it

To generate scenes, to limit free usage and unlock Pro features, to prevent abuse, and to fix problems.

Who processes it

  • Google (Gemini API and Google Cloud) processes prompts to generate scenes and hosts our server.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription status.
  • Google Play processes payments.

We do not sell your data.

Your videos

Videos are rendered and stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our server. You decide where to share them.

Retention and deletion

Prompts are not stored by our server after the scene is generated, apart from standard short-lived logs. To request deletion of any data linked to your anonymous ID, email riyazanesur.mca@gmail.com with the app version and we will act within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and change the date above.
